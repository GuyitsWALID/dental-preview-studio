### Why this is the best fit—not simply the highest rating
The selection balances web need and commercial fit, not rating alone. The spreadsheet's two highest scoring entries, Mina Dent (4.9/68, score 100) and Dr. Milky Derara (5.0/109, score 100), have publicly readable standalone sites at [minadentdentalclinic.com](https://minadentdentalclinic.com/home/) and [drmilkyderara.com](https://drmilkyderara.com/). Harla's current site ([harla.co](https://www.harla.co/)) includes booking and membership features; Green Life has an active owned site ([greenlifethiopia.com](https://www.greenlifethiopia.com/)). These retrieved sites illustrate why the spreadsheet's website-gap labels needed current checking. OLI scores 98.8 and rates 4.9/5 in the supplied workbook—close to the top entries—while its public directory still points to Facebook rather than a standalone site, and its official social channels remain active.

### Commercial qualification and caveats
Active offers, regular social publishing, contact lines, and a sizable review base are **indirect commercial-fit signals**: the clinic is doing patient acquisition and may see value in a web channel. Public information does **not** establish its budget, ability to pay, procurement authority, or willingness to buy; those remain unknown until a conversation with the decision-maker. This is a reasoned prospect recommendation, not a claim that payment is assured.

The clinic's Instagram bio states its motto as “modern dentistry, classic values.” First-party posts support the Megenagna / Grace City Mall, third-floor location and both phone numbers. The live Google Maps listing opens on OLI Dental Clinic at Kenenisa Ave, within Grace City Mall, and its share-generated iframe centers at **9.0209025, 38.7969129**. A Google listing shows Wednesday hours of 09:00–18:30 while the directory gives Monday–Saturday hours of 09:00–18:00; the contact page discloses this difference and advises calling ahead. No email address was verified. The demo now uses the supplied logo and an OLI Facebook cover photo; its generated treatment-room hero is identified as illustrative. Clinic approval should be obtained before public use. Appointments remain call/social actions with no invented booking backend.
